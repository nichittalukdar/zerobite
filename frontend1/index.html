Share Food. Share Hope.

ShareBite connects surplus food with people who need it, helping reduce food waste and build a stronger community.

What is ShareBite?

ShareBite is a platform that helps donors share available food with receivers in their community.

How It Works

1. Donate

Donors list their available food.

2. Match

ShareBite finds suitable receivers.

3. Share

Food reaches people who need it.
